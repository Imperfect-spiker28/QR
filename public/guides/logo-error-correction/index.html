Logo Embedding & Reed-Solomon Level H

How Reed-Solomon algebraic redundancy works, the mathematical 30% area limit, and why center logos never corrupt your destination payload.

The Secret Behind Logo QR Codes: Reed-Solomon Codes

In the standard QR code specification (ISO/IEC 18004), data bytes are accompanied by Reed-Solomon error correction codewords. Originally developed for deep-space telemetry and CD-ROMs, Reed-Solomon mathematics allow decoders to reconstruct damaged, smudged, or missing data blocks automatically.

When you place a logo in the center of a QR code, the camera treats that central emblem as "damaged or obscured modules" and utilizes the mathematical redundancy to solve for the original bytes.

The Four Error Correction Levels

Level Recovery Capability Suitable For
Level L (Low) Up to 7% damage recovery Clean high-density text with no logos
Level M (Medium) Up to 15% damage recovery Standard default for clean digital barcodes
Level Q (Quartile) Up to 25% damage recovery Industrial environments, small logos
Level H (High) Up to 30% damage recovery Brand logos, emblems, and harsh print conditions

The 30% Mathematical Ceiling Rule

Because Level H provides exactly 30% recovery capacity, a center logo must never occupy more than 28% to 30% of the total QR matrix surface area. Exceeding this boundary consumes 100% of the mathematical error buffer, leaving zero margin for camera glare, finger shadows, or printing scratches.

Furthermore, the logo must never overlap the three corner Finder Patterns (eye marks) or the alignment patterns located near the bottom right.

Center Logo Padding & Masking

To prevent visual interference between the logo graphic and adjacent matrix modules, a clean background buffer (masking margin) must separate the logo from the nearest dots. QRo enforces this automatically with safe margin padding and clean logo container geometry.

Automatic Safety in QRo

Whenever you upload a custom logo or keep the default Anwin emblem, QRo automatically locks the Reed-Solomon configuration to Level H and restricts the center graphic to 28% width, guaranteeing reliable scans on every test device.
