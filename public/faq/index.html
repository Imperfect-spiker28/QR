Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers on creating, customizing, testing, and downloading static QR codes with QRo.

1. How do I create a QR code for free?

Select your content type (Website Link, Plain Text, Wi-Fi Network, or Contact vCard), enter your destination information, customize patterns, colors, logos, and frame if desired, and click Download QR Code. Everything renders 100% client-side in your browser with zero registration fees.

2. Can I create a QR code without signing up?

Yes. QRo requires no account, email address, password, or credit card. All customization features, high-resolution PNG exports (up to 2048px), vector SVG files, and custom logos are immediately accessible to everyone.

3. Can I add my own logo to a QR code?

Yes. You can upload custom PNG, JPG, or SVG logos, or use the default brand monogram. QRo automatically elevates Reed-Solomon Error Correction to Level H (30% physical recovery tolerance) and constrains center emblems below 28-30% of total area with safe padding, preserving scanning reliability across all standard smartphones.

4. Can I customize QR code colors and gradients?

Yes. You can customize foreground dot modules, backgrounds, and dual-tone linear gradients using our custom color picker popover, complete with real-time scan contrast validation.

5. Which file formats can I download?

QRo exports in high-resolution raster PNG (512px, 1024px, 2048px), infinitely scalable vector SVG (ideal for print shops, merchandise, signage, and packaging), and standard JPG.

6. Can QR codes be generated offline?

Yes. QRo operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with a dedicated service worker. All calculations happen entirely within your local browser runtime via HTML5 Canvas and SVG, functioning seamlessly with zero active internet connection.

7. Is QRo free and ad-free?

Yes. QRo is open-source (MIT licensed) and 100% free with no advertisements, no paywalls, no sponsored redirect intermediaries, and no monthly subscriptions.

8. Do QRo QR codes expire?

No. QRo generates static QR codes where your content is encoded directly into the physical matrix without an intermediary redirect routing server. They do not expire due to an account tier or subscription. However, static codes cannot be changed after printing; if your URL or Wi-Fi password changes, you must generate a new code.

9. Do static QR codes provide scan analytics?

No. Static QR codes write raw payload data directly into the dots and do not inherently provide device tracking or scan counts. To measure website traffic from a printed QR code, we recommend appending standard UTM parameters (e.g. ?utm_source=qr&utm_medium=print) directly to your destination URL before generating.
